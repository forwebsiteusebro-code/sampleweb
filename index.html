Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about workshop visits, costs, and bike turnaround.

We offer comprehensive services including general bike servicing, engine repairs, oil changes, brake repairs, chain maintenance, battery checks, electrical diagnostics, and custom repair solutions for all motorcycle brands.
Our pricing varies by service type. General servicing starts from $49, oil changes from $29, and brake repairs from $35. For custom repairs, we provide detailed quotes upfront before beginning any work, so there are no surprise costs.
Booking is highly recommended to ensure your bike gets a dedicated service bay and minimal wait time. You can book through our website form, WhatsApp, or by calling us directly. We also accommodate walk-ins when schedules allow.
Service duration depends on the type of work. General servicing typically takes 1-2 hours, oil changes 30-45 minutes, and more complex repairs are quoted upfront with realistic timelines. We'll keep you updated throughout the process.
Yes, we stock OEM and quality aftermarket parts for most popular motorcycle brands. If a specific part isn't in stock, we can source it quickly while maintaining fair pricing and warranty coverage.
We service all major motorcycle brands including Triumph, Yamaha, Honda, Royal Enfield, KTM, BMW, Harley-Davidson, and more. Our technicians have multi-brand expertise and access to comprehensive service manuals.
We accept all major payment methods: cash, credit/debit cards, digital wallets, and bank transfers. Online payment options are available for upfront quotes and advance bookings.
Absolutely. We provide detailed, written quotes for all work before beginning repairs. For routine services with fixed pricing, costs are transparent upfront. Any changes discovered during the inspection are discussed with you first.
Yes! WhatsApp is our preferred contact method. You can send photos of your bike, describe issues, ask quick questions, and book appointments directly. We respond within a few hours during business hours.
